# Buying stock — what every box means (plain English)

Worked example used below: you buy **5 bags** from a mill at **6,000 a bag**. The mill gives **500 off the whole deal**.
The mill's bill also has **200 for delivery**. You pay **10,000** now.

> On the screens, every explanation is behind a small round **i** next to the label. Press it to read; press again to hide.

## The Purchase screen

| Box | What it means | In the example |
|---|---|---|
| **Rate** | The price of **ONE bag** from the mill. | 6,000 |
| **Discount** (on a line) | Money off **that whole line** (all the bags of that product together) — not per bag. It is real: it lowers what you owe. | 0 |
| **Overall discount** | Money off the **whole purchase** (all products), taken after any line discounts. It lowers what you owe **and what every bag costs**. | 500 |
| **Delivery / Loading / Other charges** | **Totals for the whole purchase — NOT per bag.** Only what the **mill charges on the same bill**. They are added to what you owe and shared over the bags. | 200 in total → 200 ÷ 5 bags = **40 per bag** |
| **Amount paid** | What you hand the mill **now**, for the **whole purchase** (all products together). The rest stays owed to the mill. | 10,000 |

Result: the bill is 5 × 6,000 − 500 + 200 = **29,700**. You owe the mill 29,700 − 10,000 = **19,700**.
Each bag costs 6,000 − 100 (500 ÷ 5) + 40 (200 ÷ 5) = **5,940**. The screen shows this live under the charges ("each bag costs …").

## Which box for which cost? (so nothing is counted twice)

There are three places for a cost of bringing goods in. **Use only ONE for the same cost.**

1. **Mill charges it on its bill** (delivery, loading) → type it in the purchase's **Delivery / Loading / Other** boxes.
2. **You pay a truck or labour yourself, every time, about the same per bag** → the product's **Extra cost per bag** (Product prices screen).
3. **A one-off cost paid to someone else for one purchase** → **Landed costs** screen.

If a product already has an Extra cost per bag and you also type charges on its purchase, the screen stops you once
("counts the same transport twice"). Pressing Save again keeps it on purpose.

## The Product prices screen

- **Purchase price** = what the mill charges per bag. It is your *reference* price. A sale is costed from what each purchase
  **really cost**, not from this box. If you have not saved one, the screen shows the mill's price from your last purchase.
- **Charges on the purchase** (shown for you, not typed here) = the delivery/loading you typed on the purchase, per bag.
- **Extra cost per bag** = your own regular transport/labour cost per bag.
- **Total cost per bag** = the three added together = what a sale will be costed at. Selling price − total cost = **profit per bag**.

## Returns and payments in one minute

- Bags coming back go **into stock automatically**. Only the money needs a choice:
  *Take it off what the shop owes* (usual) · *Give the money back in cash* (only for money the shop really paid) · *Send the same bags again*.
- If a shop paid, then returned goods, the invoice shows **Pay back N** — press it to hand the money back (you can pay part now, never more than N).
- Money received must be recorded against the **right shop** — the screen warns if that shop owes nothing.
