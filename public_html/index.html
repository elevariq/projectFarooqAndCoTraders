A regional name built on stock, not slogans

Farooq & Co Traders has operated out of Main Bazar, Dir Bala, moving rice and flour into the shops and warehouses of Chitral and Dir for years. The business runs on three warehouses, a standing supply route across the region, and direct relationships with the shopkeepers who reorder every week.

Every sack that leaves the warehouse is logged the same way it always has been — now backed by an ERP system that keeps stock, purchases and payments in one place for the office, and simple receive/dispatch screens for warehouse staff.
